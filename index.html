Video debt

you know it should be a video. it never becomes one.

Organisations have more to explain than ever. Video is the clearest way to do it, but getting a good explainer made is slow, expensive or simply wrong for the context. So the message goes out as a long email, or an old video stays in circulation because fixing it costs too much.

  • Agencies cost too much and take too long.A professional explainer costs thousands and takes four to eight weeks.

  • Every update is a new job.Change one step, one figure or one name and it means another quote and another delay.

  • DIY tools need skills most people don't have.Manual timelines, thin asset libraries and slow rendering.

  • Avatars don't fit the context.An avatar reading a CEO's message or a policy change feels wrong, and it can describe a process but can't show one.

  • Brand and approvals are hard to manage.Corporate content goes through comms, legal and leadership. Creator tools have no sign-off step.

How it works

three ways in. one clear video out.

Every route leads to the same storyboard, review and render process.

01

Start with an idea

Type what you want to explain, like "explain the hierarchy of controls to new starters". Pick a length, an audience and a tone. Explavo writes a script paced to land on time.

02

Start with a script or document

Paste your own script and Explavo breaks it into scenes and builds the visuals around your words.

PDF, Word and PowerPoint coming next
03

Start with a voice recording

Upload a real person: a CEO, a site leader, a consultant. Explavo transcribes it word by word, finds the scene breaks and times the visuals to what they say. No filming.

Review before you render

approve every scene before anything renders.

You see each scene's visuals, on-screen text and narration first, so stakeholders sign off early, when mistakes are cheap to fix.

  • Change or regenerate one scene without touching the rest
  • Only the changed scene re-renders
  • Nothing exports without sign-off

Questions

the hard questions, answered.

How do you stop the AI making things up?

Scripts are grounded in the material you give Explavo, and claims are flagged against that source. Then a person approves the storyboard before anything renders. Nothing exports without sign-off.

Will this look like generic AI video?

No. Explavo renders animation as code from tested, human-designed style templates, so the output stays consistent and on brand. You approve every scene before it renders.

We're an enterprise safety team with procurement and compliance needs.

Storyboard approval and captions are built in. Version history and SCORM packages for your LMS are coming next, and Enterprise adds SSO and Australian data hosting. Start on a single seat and roll out when you're ready.

What if a procedure or figure changes?

Change that one scene and only that scene re-renders. Updating a video is as easy as editing a document, so your content doesn't go stale.

Why not just use an avatar tool?

An avatar can describe a process but it can't show one. And a talking head reading a policy or a CEO's message feels wrong to a professional audience. Explavo shows the idea.
